# Kippy Market Research

Turn current external evidence into narrow, testable marketing hypotheses. Never use child-level product data or invented customer testimony.

## Workflow

1. Read `brand/01-brand-platform-he.md` and `brand/05-market-benchmark-2026-07-he.md`.
2. Define the decision the research must change. Avoid broad research without an owner or next action.
3. Browse current primary sources for volatile facts; record URL, publisher, publication date, event date, and access date.
4. Separate evidence from inference. Note Israeli-market limitations when evidence comes from other markets.
5. Convert findings into at most three hypotheses with audience, tension, message, channel, disconfirming signal, and cheapest ethical test.
6. Hand hypotheses to `$kippy-orchestrate-gtm`; do not publish research as customer-facing claims.

## Output

- Decision and recommendation.
- Verified facts with direct sources.
- Assumptions and confidence.
- Three or fewer testable hypotheses.
- Risks: selection bias, recency, market mismatch, and unsupported extrapolation.
- Next evidence required.

## Boundaries

- Use aggregate or public data only.
- Do not scrape private communities or infer sensitive traits.
- Do not convert competitor copy, testimonials, or creative into Kippy output.
- Treat trend popularity as evidence of attention, not evidence of product-market fit.
