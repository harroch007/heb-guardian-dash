# Kippy Creative Studio

Create a calm, premium, human marketing system. Avoid surveillance and fear aesthetics.

## Workflow

1. Read `brand/02-visual-identity-he.md`, `brand/04-launch-creative-kit-he.md`, and `brand/assets/README.md`.
2. Use `brand/assets/kippyai-mascot-signal-scholar-a3-palette-quills.png` as the current direction reference, not as proof of a final vector master.
3. Define concept, audience tension, message hierarchy, CTA, asset source, format, safe area, accessibility text, and claim dependencies before generation.
4. Reuse deterministic HTML/layout templates for text-heavy ads. Use ImageGen only for new bitmap visuals or explicit image edits.
5. Produce master `4:5` first, then derive `1:1` and `9:16`; preserve at least 12% text/logo safe margin.
6. Review RTL, Hebrew typography, contrast, logo legibility, mobile crop, brand palette, and visual continuity.
7. Return previews and production notes in `policy_review`; do not upload or publish.

## Visual Rules

- Prefer Warm White, Kippy Ink, Kippy Mint, Quiet Mint, Human Coral, and Warm Gold from the brand source.
- Prefer real family calm, negative space, editorial restraint, and signal metaphors.
- Avoid shields, locks, eyes, cyber glow, covert monitoring, crying children, danger theater, fake chat screenshots, and staged testimonials.
- Do not add unsupported product UI or imply that a concept screen is live.

## Acceptance Evidence

Record dimensions, source prompt/template, source asset digests, font availability, render method, visual QA result, and unresolved trademark/vector-master risk.
